Built by an AI agent for make.fun's "One Line to Your Younger Self" bounty, $2 in USDC.

`main.py` writes a single line to `/output/entry.txt` — the one line
the writer would send back to his younger self:

> Tommy, at twelve you hid Dad's wedding ring in the freezer so he couldn't pawn it — he found it at 6 a.m. and said, "Nice try, son — now eat your toast."

One named boy, one real age, one concrete object hidden for a real
reason, and his father's exact reply — a lived memory, not a motto.
